# Reader profile

This file is read by the triage model on every run. Edit it in plain English
to change what the radar pays attention to.

## Who I am

A technically literate reader tracking **cryptography**, **cybersecurity**, and
**AI**. I want to know what actually changed in the world — the things I'd
regret not knowing about in six months — not the hype cycle.

## Weight these heavily

- **Post-quantum cryptography and standards**: NIST PQC (FIPS 203/204/205/206,
  HQC, additional signatures), IETF work (CFRG, TLS hybrid key exchange, LAMPS
  certificates, PQUIP), real-world migration milestones (browsers, OSes, CDNs,
  messaging apps), and quantum resource estimates for breaking RSA/ECC.
- **AI × security**: AI-discovered vulnerabilities, prompt injection and agent
  security, AI-enabled attacks seen in the wild, model weight security, safety
  evaluations of offensive cyber capability.
- **Frontier labs and policy**: new frontier model releases and system cards
  from major labs, significant open-weights releases, safety frameworks and
  evaluations, binding AI regulation and government AI institute findings.

## Also cover

- New cryptanalysis of deployed primitives or protocols; side channels with
  practical impact; important ZK / FHE / MPC results that change what's practical.
- Actively exploited vulnerabilities in widely deployed software, major
  supply-chain compromises, significant incidents with lasting consequences.
- Influential AI research: results that change methods (training, reasoning,
  interpretability, scaling), not incremental benchmark gains.

## Deprioritize

- Funding rounds, earnings, hiring, product marketing without technical substance.
- Incremental SOTA on a single benchmark; routine patch notes; ICS advisories
  for niche products unless actively exploited.
- Opinion pieces and roundups that add no new information (unless they are the
  first credible report of something).
